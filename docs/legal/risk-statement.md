# Risk statement

**Version 2026-10-20 · Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer**

Please read this before you sign up. It explains, in plain words, what Amanat does and does not do, and the risks you keep as the person running the business. It is part of our Terms of Use.

## Amanat keeps records. It does not hold money.

- Every amount in Amanat (cash, bank and mobile money balances, amanat held for clients, profit) is a **record that you or your team entered**. Amanat never holds, moves or guarantees any of that money.
- The real money is wherever you keep it: your cash drawer, your bank, your M-Pesa or other accounts. If the records and the real money disagree, **the real money is what counts**. Count it at every closing.

## Your figures are only as good as what is entered

- Balances, profit and reports are calculated from the records. A wrong amount, rate or account typed in gives a wrong result.
- Anyone you give an Owner or Editor role can add and change records. Give those roles only to people you trust, and check the Activity log.
- Profit figures use the rates set in the App and the method described in it. They are not tax calculations or audited accounts.

## Rates

- Reference market rates shown in the App come from a public source, may be late or wrong, and are a guide only. **The buy and sell rates you set are your decision.**
- Currency values change quickly. You carry the risk of any trade you make.

## Amanat held for clients

- Recording an amanat does not protect it. You are responsible for keeping the client's money safe and giving it back.

## The service can fail

- Like any online service, Amanat can be slow, unavailable or lose connection, and data can be lost through a fault, an attack or a mistake. **Keep your own records of anything you would need if the App were unavailable**, for example your daily closing reports.
- Keep your password secret. Anyone with it can see and change your business's records.

## Your legal duties stay yours

- You are responsible for your licences (for example from the Central Bank of Kenya), for knowing your customers, for reporting suspicious transactions where the law requires it, and for your taxes. Amanat does not do these for you, and using it does not mean you comply.
